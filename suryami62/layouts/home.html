A very unofficial law of physics

The Conservation of Side Projects

In a closed system, unfinished side projects are neither created nor destroyed. They simply change state:

  1. idea
  2. new repository
  3. “I’ll finish this weekend”
  4. archived but not forgotten

No known escape velocity.
